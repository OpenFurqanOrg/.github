### OpenFurqan: Quran App — Android

- Read verse by verse, or page by page as in a printed Mushaf
- Translations in 75 languages and 44 tafsirs, from Ibn Kathir to Maarif-ul-Quran
- Tap any word for its meaning and root, with all 1,651 Quranic roots
- Hadith linked to the ayahs they explain
- 16 reciters, with the text following the recitation
- Memorize with daily review, and learn Quran vocabulary

### OpenFurqan on the web

The Quran in your browser at [openfurqan.com](https://openfurqan.com): reader, tafsir, word meanings and memorization.

### What we hold to

- **Content first.** The Quran leads; the interface stays quiet.
- **Private by design.** No ads, no account, no data collected.
- **Sources credited.** Every translation, tafsir and dataset is credited to its author and publisher.
